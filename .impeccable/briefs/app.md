# Surface brief: the Prepify app (all signed-in screens)

Mode: Operate. Phone first, then laptop, desktop and tablet.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Practice is a journey across a map, not a dashboard of cards. Home is one subject's chapters drawn as stops on a winding path; it refuses the stat-card grid and the hero-metric template.

OWN-WORLD: Pale sky (#EAF6FF), sky-to-grass gradients on the map, white surfaces with a solid lifted edge (0 4px 0), chunky press-able buttons that sink when tapped, Prepi purple (#6C4CF5) for selection and primary actions, orange (#E85D0C) only for the big play buttons, gold (#FFC93C) for stars and finished stops. Baloo 2 for every heading, button and number; Nunito for reading. Prepi, a purple blob with a gold antenna star, appears where a person would.

STORY: A student opens the app, sees exactly where they are in a subject, taps the glowing stop and is in a ten-question test in two taps; results pay out in stars that light up the map.

FIRST VIEWPORT (phone): greeting and next-stop line; subject pills; the map panel with the subject banner and the first stops, the next stop glowing with Prepi beside it; tab bar at the bottom. Primary action: the glowing stop.

FORM: Level map (direction O, chosen by the owner from 14 picture directions). seed: none (unavailable)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
